One song, every language, perfectly in sync. The infrastructure for a borderless music industry.

  • One song, every language.

    Re‑sung, not translated. The hook lands the same in a language it was never written in.

  • The voice stays the voice.

    Tone, phrasing, feeling. It still sounds like them, because it is.

  • Nothing moves without consent.

    Artists and rights holders approve every second before anything ships.

From one song to every language, in four steps.

  1. 1

    You send the song.

    The original recording is where every version starts.

  2. 2

    The lyrics are rewritten to be sung.

    Not translated word for word. Matched to the melody, the rhythm and the feeling, the way a native songwriter would write it.

  3. 3

    It’s sung in the original voice.

    The new vocal keeps the artist’s tone and phrasing, in sync with the original performance.

  4. 4

    Nothing ships until you approve it.

    Artists and rights holders hear every line and sign off before anything is released.
